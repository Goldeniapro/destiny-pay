What this demo is

Destiny Pay is a hackathon demo that runs on the PayPal Sandbox: no real money moves. Each visitor gets a sample business with invented customers and suppliers. There are no user accounts. Destiny Pay is not affiliated with or endorsed by PayPal.

What is stored

  • Your sample business (contacts, invoices, bills, reminders, notes) in the demo's database, identified only by a random 128-bit ID kept in your browser.
  • Your browser keeps that ID, your language and your mute setting in local storage. No cookies are used.
  • Server logs record errors, not page visits.

Who processes what you say or type

  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your messages and your sample business's data to understand requests and prepare actions.
  • PayPal (Sandbox) receives the invoices and test payouts you authorize.
  • Fish Audio turns Destiny's replies into speech and, if your browser can't dictate, transcribes your recorded voice. Otherwise your browser's own speech recognition is used.

Please don't enter real personal or financial data: this is a demo.

Retention and deletion

Inactive sample businesses are deleted automatically. To start over at any time, use “New sample business”. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
